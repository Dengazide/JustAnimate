# Quality gates

- Fact-check dates, numbers, cause and consequences; qualify disputed details.
- Every third-party asset has provenance and a compatible licence.
- Exact duration, fps, 9:16 aspect ratio and requested resolution.
- No unintended blank, black, white, frozen or pop frames.
- Character costumes and props remain consistent and readable.
- Narration is intelligible; audio is 48 kHz; measure integrated loudness and true peak.
- Keep contact sheets and QA report beside the render.
- State whether the result is a storyboard preview, a tested Three.js render, or a final Blender/Three.js render.
